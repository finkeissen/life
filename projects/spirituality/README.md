# Project: spirituality

**Projection space within the `life` architecture – survey of personal meaning-making.**

This project maps the broad field of **personal spirituality**: individual ways of creating orientation, depth and meaning within the constraints of finite, irreversible life — with or without reference to the transcendent.

### Core Determination
Spirituality is treated here as **any sustained personal practice or orientation** that helps a person relate meaningfully to existence, suffering, finitude, joy and responsibility — whether religious, secular, nature-based, artistic or psychological.

It does **not** require direct mystical experience or adherence to a religious institution.

### Key Aspects Investigated
- Personal practices (meditation, mindfulness, journaling, service, creativity, nature connection, etc.)
- Individual meaning-making strategies under life-invariants
- Secular, humanistic and post-religious forms of spirituality
- Integration of transrational dimensions into daily existence
- Recurring patterns (e.g. gratitude, acceptance, compassion, awe)
- Tension between personal depth and everyday constraints
- Modern developments (mindfulness culture, wellness spirituality, eco-spirituality, etc.)

### Approach
Emphasis on documentation and comparison of existing spiritual approaches. Creative intensification is secondary.

### Relation to BEING, Religions and Mysticism
- **BEING** examines the structural invariants themselves.  
- **Religions** provide collective frameworks.  
- **Mysticism** focuses on rare, intense breakthroughs.  
- **Spirituality** surveys the **broad, everyday personal repertoire** people actually use to live meaningfully.

Spirituality is thus the **practical, individual layer** — the most accessible and widespread.

### Projection Discipline
Declared reduction: mapping and analysis rather than new projection. Core architecture remains untouched.
