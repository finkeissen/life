# Characters

## Ekkehard (Medieval)

A monk associated with St. Gallen.

Represents immersion into institutional symbolic order.

## Meister Eckhart

Mystical theologian challenging doctrinal boundaries.

Represents breakthrough insight.

## Ekkehard (Modern)

Contemporary intellectual navigating institutional systems.

Represents the modern recurrence of the epistemic trajectory.

## The Institution

Embodied by abbots, theologians, administrators, professors.

Represents structural authority.

## The Chorus

Represents society, tradition, and collective belief.
