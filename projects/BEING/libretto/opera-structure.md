# Opera Structure (Libretto)

The opera condenses the architecture of BEING into a symbolic dramatic form.

## Act I — Disturbance

Focus:
The first visible crack in the assumed moral order.

Characters:
- Ekkehard
- Friend
- Teacher / institutional voice
- Chorus (society)

Musical function:
Introduce tension between order and emerging contradiction.

---

## Act II — Confrontation

Focus:
Irreversible decisions and exposure of responsibility.

Characters:
- Ekkehard
- Institutional authority
- Partner
- Mirror character
- Chorus

Musical function:
Amplify emotional and ethical pressure.

---

## Act III — Recognition

Focus:
Loss of moral relief and structural recognition.

Characters:
- Ekkehard
- Witness
- Chorus

Musical function:
Transform recognition into collective reflection.

The opera ends without final resolution.
Recognition replaces redemption.
