# Materials — BEING

This directory contains raw material related to the **BEING** projection.

The purpose of this space is to collect and preserve ideas, references,
and fragments before they are shaped into actual work.

Material stored here is **not yet part of the work itself**.

It becomes part of the work only after it is transformed into scenes,
structures, characters, or other projection artifacts.

---

## Function

`materials/` acts as a workspace for gathering and exploring input that may
later feed the project.

Typical content includes:

- external sources
- personal ideas
- conceptual notes
- narrative fragments
- observations
- motifs or themes

This space allows ideas to exist in an early and unfinished form
without forcing them immediately into the structure of the work.

---

## Suggested Structure


materials/
sources/
ideas/
fragments/
notes/


### sources
External material that may inform the project.

Examples:

- books
- articles
- historical material
- documents
- interviews

---

### ideas
Conceptual thoughts related to the project.

Examples:

- character ideas
- narrative possibilities
- world elements
- thematic directions
- structural ideas

---

### fragments
Small pieces of creative material that are not yet part of a full scene.

Examples:

- lines of dialogue
- scene fragments
- visual images
- motifs
- short narrative sketches

---

### notes
Working notes and reflections.

Examples:

- thoughts about characters
- questions about structure
- reflections on the framework
- observations that may become material later

---

## Guiding Principle

Material in this directory is intentionally **unfinished**.

It should not be forced into premature structure.

Only when material becomes part of a scene, character, or narrative
structure does it move from **materials** into the actual projection.
