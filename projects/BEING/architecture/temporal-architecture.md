# Temporal Architecture

The narrative spans approximately one millennium.

Time is not treated as linear progression
but as structural resonance.

Three temporal layers interact:

1. Present Layer  
   The modern narrator (Ekkehard).

2. Historical Layer  
   Figures named Ekkehard / Eckart appearing in different centuries.

3. Reflective Layer  
   Moments in which the narrator reflects on the chain itself.

Transitions between layers may occur through:

- dreams
- manuscripts
- historical research
- sudden recognition
- unexplained shifts

Temporal order is intentionally unstable.

The recurrence of similar conflicts across centuries
reveals structural continuity under changing conditions.
