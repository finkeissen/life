# Character System

## Principle

Characters in BEING are defined first by structural function and only
secondarily by biography.

The narrative purpose of a character is to reveal how individuals
participate in structural conditions.

---

## Core Character Functions

The following functions are recurrent across projections:

1. Origin of order  
   Figures introducing the early moral and social framework
   (e.g., parents, early teachers).

2. Alternative path  
   Individuals living under similar conditions but making different choices.

3. Institutional meaning  
   Representatives of organizations, traditions, or formal authority.

4. Emotional proximity  
   Figures who create personal attachment and vulnerability
   (friends, partners).

5. Structural mirror  
   A character whose trajectory parallels Ekkehard’s but diverges
   at crucial moments.

6. Witness  
   An observer who reflects or records events without controlling them.

7. Next generation  
   Characters who inherit the structures revealed by Ekkehard’s life.

---

## Reuse Principle

A single character may fulfill multiple functions across different
phases of the life timeline.

Example:

Friend → later colleague → later mirror figure

This reuse reinforces structural recurrence within the narrative.

---

## Character Economy

The project should prefer:

- fewer characters with multiple functions
over
- many characters with narrow roles.
