# Life Age Axis

Each Ekkehard figure appears at a specific stage of life.
Insight does not necessarily correlate with age.

Possible stages:

Childhood – absorbed order  
Youth – first irritation  
Early adulthood – intellectual awakening  
Midlife – confrontation with responsibility  
Late life – exposure without resolution

Important rule:
A young Ekkehard may perceive structural truth earlier
than an older one in another century.
Age does not guarantee wisdom.
