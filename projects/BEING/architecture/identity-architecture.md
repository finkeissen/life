# Identity Architecture

Identity across the narrative is intentionally unstable.

The recurrence of Ekkehard figures may represent:

- continuity of consciousness
- spiritual inheritance
- intellectual temperament
- structural role repeated across generations

The work avoids confirming any single explanation.

What persists across centuries may instead be:

a pattern of confrontation with responsibility.

Identity therefore functions less as personal continuity
and more as structural resonance.

The modern narrator does not claim to be these figures.

Yet he cannot fully separate himself from them.
