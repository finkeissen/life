# Historical Time Axis

This axis represents the macro-historical development across roughly 1000 years.

It tracks the external world in which the Ekkehard figures exist: institutions,
cosmologies, political systems, and cultural assumptions.

Example phases:

1000 – Monastic order, cosmic hierarchy assumed  
1200 – Mystical interiority and theological tension  
1350 – Institutional pressure and doctrinal control  
1500 – Fragmentation of religious authority  
1700 – Rational system building and early modern state  
1900 – Existential fracture and collapse of inherited order  
Present – Structural awareness without stable metaphysical ground

Important rule:
Historical time must not imply moral or intellectual progress.
Earlier centuries may contain deeper insight than later ones.
