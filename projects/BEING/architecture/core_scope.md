# Core Scope — life

The purpose of this file is to clarify what actually belongs to the core
and what should only emerge within projections.

The core is not a complete model of the world.
It defines only the structural conditions under which projections operate.

---

## Level 1 — Invariants

Structural properties of human existence
that are treated as stable within the framework.

Examples:

- World > consciousness  
- Irreversibility of decisions  
- Non-delegable responsibility  
- Development under constraint  
- Structural failure

These elements are expected to remain stable.

If a projection collapses at this level,
the core itself may require revision.

---

## Level 2 — Models

Conceptual structures used to organize or describe invariants.

Examples:

- Models of decision situations
- Models of development
- Models of responsibility
- Structural dynamics of conflict

These may be refined over time.

If projections consistently reveal blind spots,
models may be adjusted.

---

## Level 3 — Interpretations

Concrete realizations within specific media.

Examples:

- Narrative forms
- Character constellations
- Specific worlds
- Cultural settings

This level does **not** belong to the core.
It emerges only within projections.

---

## Guiding Rule

Core work ends where concrete situations,
characters, or stories begin.

From that point onward, the work becomes projection.
