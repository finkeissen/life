# Media Allocation

This document clarifies how the shared architecture of BEING
can be expressed differently across narrative media.

Each medium emphasizes different parts of the life timeline.

---

## Novel

Strengths:
- psychological nuance
- extended reflection
- detailed life chronology

Usage:
- can cover the full life timeline
- explores internal recognition processes
- provides historical and relational context

---

## Film

Strengths:
- situational intensity
- visual environments
- compressed time structures

Usage:
- focuses on a limited period (often Phase III or IV)
- may include flashbacks to earlier phases
- emphasizes decisive events and consequences

---

## Libretto / Opera

Strengths:
- emotional concentration
- symbolic condensation
- musical amplification of recognition

Usage:
- selects a few decisive moments
- typically structured in three acts:

Act I — emergence of disturbance (Phase II)  
Act II — confrontation with responsibility (Phase III)  
Act III — loss of moral relief (Phase IV)

Chorus may function as structural witness or collective voice.

---

## Shared Rule

No projection may contradict the narrative ontology of the project.
Each medium reveals a different aspect of the same structural conditions.
