# Scene Structure (Novel)

Scenes in the novel should follow structural recognition rather than traditional conflict escalation.

## Scene Elements

1. Situation
A stable or apparently understandable situation.

2. Disruption
A small contradiction appears.

3. Recognition
A character realizes something cannot be delegated or explained away.

4. Consequence
The situation changes irreversibly.

5. Continuation
Life proceeds without resolution.

---

## Scene Rule

Scenes should avoid:

- heroic triumph
- moral purification
- total explanation

Scenes should reveal:

- responsibility
- structural participation
- epistemic shifts
