# Character Presence

## Purpose

This document defines how characters should appear within the film projection.

Characters are not primarily psychological containers.
They are loci in which structural tensions become visible.

## Presence Modes

### Embedded Presence

The character is shown as part of an existing order.

### Deviating Presence

The character begins to register inconsistency.

### Pressured Presence

The character is visibly constrained by institutional or social systems.

### Reflective Presence

The character pauses, observes, or reinterprets.

### Isolated Presence

The character remains perceptible while losing social integration.

## Constraint

Performance should avoid over-expressive psychology.
The aim is not explanation of the self but visibility of structural relation.
