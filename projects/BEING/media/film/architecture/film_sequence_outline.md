# Film Sequence Outline

## Purpose

This document proposes a minimal structural outline for a film projection.

It does not prescribe plot.
It defines sequence-level movement.

## Outline

### Sequence 1 — Order

A coherent institutional world is established.

### Sequence 2 — Fracture

Small inconsistencies disturb the local order.

### Sequence 3 — Repetition

Analogous situations recur in another context or epoch.

### Sequence 4 — Pressure

Institutions respond defensively to emergent insight.

### Sequence 5 — Reorganization

Earlier events become newly legible.

### Sequence 6 — Isolation

Structural awareness produces distance from the collective order.

### Sequence 7 — Unstable Equilibrium

A new understanding exists without full reconciliation.

## Constraint

This outline may be linear, recursive, or cross-epochal,
but each sequence must preserve its structural function.
