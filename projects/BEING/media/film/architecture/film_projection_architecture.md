# Film Projection Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the film-specific projection of the narrative architecture.

Film does not reproduce the novel or the libretto.
It instantiates the same structural system through cinematic means.

## Projection Basis

The film projection derives from:

- the virtual timeline
- the structural tension matrix
- the structural mirror logic
- the recurrence engine
- the dramaturgy layer

## Filmic Priorities

Within film, the following dimensions move into the foreground:

- spatial arrangement
- bodies under pressure
- environmental constraint
- repeated institutional situations
- temporal fragmentation
- visible but not fully explained recurrence

## Filmic Reduction

Film reduces:

- direct access to thought
- explicit conceptual exposition
- stable authorial explanation

Film increases:

- atmospheric density
- relational ambiguity
- structural pressure through image and sound

## Projection Principle

The film should not explain the architecture.
It should allow the architecture to become perceptible.

## Working Rule

Every sequence must answer:
What structural tension, recurrence pattern, or epistemic shift becomes visible here through cinematic form?
