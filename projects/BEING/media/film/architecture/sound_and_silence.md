# Sound and Silence

## Purpose

This document defines the sonic projection of structural tension.

Sound should not simply support emotion.
It should register:

- institutional atmospheres
- procedural repetition
- spatial pressure
- interruption
- isolation

## Sound Functions

### Institutional Sound

Administrative routines, liturgical order, mechanical repetition, ambient control.

### Fracture Sound

An interruption, omission, or altered sonic pattern revealing instability.

### Recurrence Sound

Repeated sonic elements appearing across contexts.

### Silence

Used not as emptiness but as structural exposure.

### Residual Sound

Sound continuing after action to reveal institutional persistence.

## Music Constraint

Music must not resolve ambiguity too early.
It should support structural perception rather than emotional instruction.
