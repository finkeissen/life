# Film

This directory hosts **film** as a projection of the BEING architecture.

Film is not treated here as a neutral adaptation medium.
It is understood as a structurally specific projection with distinct strengths, blind spots, and formal constraints.

Film emphasizes:

- simultaneity
- environment
- contingency
- relational dynamics
- visual recurrence
- temporal compression

Typical strengths:

- situational density
- contextual realism
- non-verbal relation
- atmosphere and environmental pressure
- layering of visible and implicit structures

Typical blind spots:

- false coherence through continuity
- premature emotional identification
- over-personalization of structural tension
- weak explicit handling of epistemic abstraction
- closure pressure through conventional narrative form

Structural constraint:

Film carries a high risk of false coherence.

Any film project must explicitly state:

- how narrative closure is resisted
- how structural recurrence becomes visible
- how institutions are shown without becoming caricature
- how insight appears without explanatory overstatement

## Workflow Logic

1. Define film-specific projection constraints.
2. Translate the virtual timeline into filmic sequence architecture.
3. Define visual, sonic, and temporal recurrence.
4. Build scenes only after structural functions are fixed.

No cinematic scene invention without architectural reference.
