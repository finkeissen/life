# Shot Logic

## Purpose

This document defines how shots should function structurally.

Shots are not only units of coverage.
They are units of relation.

## Shot Functions

### Orientation Shots

Establish institutional and spatial order.

### Deviation Shots

Introduce a detail that disturbs coherence.

### Recurrence Shots

Repeat a framing or gesture under altered conditions.

### Pressure Shots

Show bodies constrained by systems or spaces.

### Threshold Shots

Mark transitions between interpretive states.

### Residual Shots

Remain after action has ended to register structural consequence.

## Editing Principle

Shot order should intensify interpretation, not merely document action.

## Constraint

Coverage logic should be minimized.
Shots should be selected by structural function rather than generic continuity.
