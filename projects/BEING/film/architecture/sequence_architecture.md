# Sequence Architecture

## Purpose

This document translates the virtual timeline into filmic sequence logic.

The film should be built from structural sequences rather than purely plot-driven scenes.

## Sequence Types

### Immersion Sequence

Establishes a stable local order.

### Fracture Sequence

Introduces an anomaly or inconsistency.

### Recognition Sequence

Shows recurrence across situations or times.

### Conflict Sequence

Makes tension between insight and stability socially visible.

### Insight Sequence

Reorganizes the meaning of previous sequences.

### Isolation Sequence

Shows the cost of structural awareness.

### Reconfiguration Sequence

Establishes a new but unstable equilibrium.

## Structural Progression

A film may organize these sequences:

- linearly
- recursively
- in mirrored clusters across epochs

## Working Rule

A sequence is valid only if it changes how surrounding sequences are interpreted.
