# Film Outline

The film version concentrates on a limited period of Ekkehard's life,
typically Phase III or Phase IV of the life timeline.

## Focus

- institutional pressure
- situational decisions
- visible consequences

The film emphasizes present-tense tension rather than full biography.

---

## Structure

### Opening
A stable professional or social situation.

### Disruption
An event reveals hidden structural consequences.

### Escalation
Attempts to manage responsibility through institutional logic fail.

### Recognition
Ekkehard realizes responsibility cannot be delegated.

### Aftermath
The world continues, but interpretation has changed.

---

## Visual Emphasis

Film should highlight:

- environments and institutions
- physical presence of decisions
- reactions of others

Recognition is expressed through situation rather than explanation.
