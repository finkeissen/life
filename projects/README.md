# Projects

Projects are **projection spaces** within the `life` architecture.

They do not define the core.  
They do not complete it.  
They do not resolve it.

Each project is a bounded and declared reduction
through which certain structural aspects become more visible,
while others are necessarily underweighted, displaced, or lost.

Projects are therefore not additive supplements to the architecture.
They are partial, selective, and loss-bearing realizations.

---

## Status of the Project Layer

The project layer is the space in which the architecture
is forced into concrete form.

Here, structural assumptions are no longer held only at the level of principle,
but tested through projection, reduction, medium, and execution.

Projects are therefore neither illustrations
nor applications in a narrow sense.

They are disciplined exposures.

A project is successful
not when it is complete,
and not when it is aesthetically impressive,
but when it remains structurally honest:
consistent with the architecture,
aware of its own reduction,
and explicit about its blindness.

---

## Relation to the Core

Projects are derived from the core architecture of `life`.

They may restate selected structural assumptions locally
in order to remain readable on their own,
but they do not establish independent theory.

Core terminology should remain compatible with the shared architectural vocabulary.
Projects may sharpen or intensify terms in local use,
but should not silently redefine them.

The project layer therefore allows controlled overlap,
but not conceptual drift.

---

## Purpose of the Project Layer

The project layer exists to:

- test the architecture under concrete projection
- expose what becomes visible under specific reduction
- reveal blind spots, omissions, and distortions
- compare how different realizations carry or fail to carry order
- make explicit which dimensions remain insufficiently projected

A project is not required to be balanced.
It is required to declare its imbalance.

---

## What Every Project Must State

Each project folder must contain a `README.md`
that makes the following explicit:

- what the project is
- which structural aspects it foregrounds
- which aspects it intensifies or overstates
- which aspects it leaves aside or underweights
- which formal constraints apply within the project
- how recurring figures, avatars, or equivalent carriers function structurally
- what current realization status the project has

Projects that do not perform this self-positioning
remain structurally incomplete.

---

## Allowed Overlap

Project READMEs are allowed to repeat selected assumptions
from the surrounding architecture.

This overlap is not considered a defect
as long as it serves project readability
and does not claim independent foundational authority.

In this repository, a project should be understandable
without requiring constant upward reference.
For that reason, local restatement is permitted.

The rule is therefore:

**repetition is allowed; silent redefinition is not.**

---

## Medium and Realization

Projects are not identical with media categories.

A project may realize itself through one medium
or through several.

Possible media include, among others:

- novel
- opera
- libretto
- film
- essay
- installation
- performance

These are not project folders by necessity.
They are possible realization forms.

The same project may move across media
without ceasing to be the same project,
provided that its structural invariants remain legible.

A change of medium does not justify
a change of core project logic.

## Current Project Status

At present, the main explicit project in this layer is:

### `BEING/`

A projection centered on recurrence,
non-redemptive responsibility,
irreversible exposure,
and repetition without guaranteed resolution.

Its current principal realization is the **Ekkehard cycle**,
including novel, libretto, and opera forms.

`BEING` should be read as a project-space,
not merely as a genre container
and not as a single finished work.

Further projects may be added later.
Their absence should not be mistaken for irrelevance;
it marks the current incompleteness of projection.

---

## Known Gaps Across the Current Project Layer

Independent of any one project,
the following aspects are not yet sufficiently projected:

- structural interest conflicts
- mundane, non-dramatic adequacy
- quiet forms of maturity
- failure without catastrophe
- development without explicit narrative arc
- sustained misalignment without resolution
- embodied coexistence outside tragic intensification

These gaps are not merely accidental.
They indicate where the present project layer
remains narrow,
and where future projections may become necessary.

---

## Non-Canonical Status

No project is definitive.

Multiple projects may contradict one another
without contradicting the architecture.

A project may remain fragmentary,
be superseded,
or be abandoned
without damage to the core.

The architecture does not depend
on the permanence of any single projection.

---

## Reminder

Projects are not answers.

They are exposures.

What is not projected remains structurally important.
What is repeatedly unprojected becomes a signal.
