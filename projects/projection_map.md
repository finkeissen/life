# Projection Map

## Purpose

This document illustrates how different media may express the same underlying architecture.

The correspondences described here are not prescriptions.
They describe possible structural alignments between projections.

Each medium remains formally independent.


---

# Architectural Basis

All projections derive from the same structural elements:

- the virtual timeline
- the structural tension matrix
- the structural mirror logic
- the recurrence engine
- the dramaturgy layer


---

# Cross-Medium Correspondence

| Structural Element | Novel | Libretto | Film |
|---|---|---|---|
| Virtual Timeline | narrative phases | operatic acts or cycles | sequence architecture |
| Structural Tension | internal and social conflict | vocal and orchestral tension | spatial and institutional pressure |
| Structural Mirror | mirrored scenes across epochs | leitmotif and dramatic mirroring | visual recurrence and montage |
| Recurrence Engine | repeated narrative situations | musical recurrence | repeated framing, gestures, situations |
| Insight | reinterpretation through narration | aria, ensemble rupture, silence | retrospective image relation |
| Institution | narrative environment | chorus and role structures | architecture, procedures, routines |


---

# Interpretation

These correspondences describe structural tendencies rather than fixed translations.

Different projections may emphasize different aspects of the architecture.


---

# Working Rule

Structural alignment should remain visible, but never forced.

A projection becomes invalid if it sacrifices the logic of its own medium in order to imitate another.
