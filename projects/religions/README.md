# Project: religions

**Projection space within the `life` architecture – investigation of existing collective perspectives.**

This project examines existing religious traditions, institutions, dogmas, rituals and belief systems as **collective, historically evolved Sichtweisen** on reality, order, meaning and human coexistence.

### Core Determination
Religions are understood here as **cultural-technological systems** that attempt to:
- provide shared answers to the fundamental questions of existence (origin, suffering, death, morality, purpose)
- stabilize community, morality and social order under the constraints of finite, irreversible life
- mediate between the individual and the transcendent (or the sacred) through collective forms

Religions are primarily **collective phenomena** — even when they contain strong individual or mystical currents.

### Key Aspects Investigated
- Dogmatic and doctrinal cores (axioms, creeds, holy scriptures, official teachings)
- Institutional and social structures (churches, priesthoods, legal systems, orthodoxy vs. heresy)
- Rituals, practices and calendars (collective worship, rites of passage, festivals)
- Ethical and moral frameworks (guilt, responsibility, justice, social order)
- Cosmologies and eschatologies (creation, afterlife, end times)
- Stabilizing and destabilizing effects on individuals and societies
- Tension between collective form and individual experience
- Historical development, schisms, syncretism and secularization

### Approach
Emphasis on sober comparative documentation and structural analysis of what already exists. Creative intensification is deliberately minimized.

### Relation to BEING, Mysticism and Spirituality
- **BEING** examines the raw structural recurrence of unresolved responsibility and human interaction.  
- **Religions** surveys the **collective belief systems and institutions** humans have built to interpret and manage that reality.  
- **Mysticism** focuses on **direct, non-discursive personal encounters** with the transcendent.  
- **Spirituality** maps **individual, often non-institutional meaning-making practices**.

Religions thus form the **collective layer**.

### Projection Discipline
Declared reduction: observation, comparison and structural breakdown. No redefinition of life-invariants. No attempt to create a “better” religion.
