# Invariants — life

Invariants are structural properties of human existence
that the framework treats as stable conditions.

They are not empirical laws
and not metaphysical claims.

They are working assumptions
about the structural constraints under which human life unfolds.

If a projection repeatedly fails at the level of an invariant,
the framework itself may require revision.

---

## Core Invariants

### World > Consciousness

Human consciousness does not determine the structure of the world.

The world places constraints on perception,
action, and interpretation.

Meaning cannot be constructed independently
of the world's structure.

---

### Irreversibility of Decisions

Decisions alter the structure of the future.

Once enacted, many decisions cannot be undone.
Their consequences propagate through time.

Human development therefore occurs under irreversible conditions.

---

### Non-Delegable Responsibility

Certain decisions cannot be fully transferred
to institutions, systems, or other individuals.

Responsibility ultimately returns to the acting subject.

Even when authority is distributed,
responsibility cannot be eliminated.

---

### Development Under Constraint

Human development does not occur in unlimited freedom.

It unfolds within constraints such as:

- time
- knowledge
- resources
- social structures
- biological limits

Growth always occurs within limitation.

---

### Structural Failure

Failure is not merely accidental.

Certain forms of failure are structurally unavoidable
within complex human situations.

Systems, relationships, and intentions
can break down even under rational or ethical action.

---

## Function of Invariants

Invariants provide the structural pressure
under which projections operate.

They should appear within works
not as theoretical statements,
but as conditions shaping situations, choices, and outcomes.
